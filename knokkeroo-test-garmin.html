knokkeroo for Garmin

knokkeroo is not available on Garmin watches

Garmin will not list knokkeroo on the Connect IQ store. We submitted it on 3 August 2026 and it was declined the same day.

The reason was not a fault in the app, and it is not something we can fix and try again. Garmin does not allow apps whose purpose is letting people know you are okay — they place them in a category they keep off the store altogether. They read our explanation that knokkeroo is not an emergency or safety service, and declined anyway.

So there is nothing to install and nothing to test on a Garmin watch, and we are not asking anyone to try. knokkeroo works on your phone, and on Apple Watch, Wear OS and Apple TV — please pick your device here instead.
