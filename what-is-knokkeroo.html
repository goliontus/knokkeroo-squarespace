What is knokkeroo?

knokkeroo is one button. You tap it, and everyone watching over you — a partner, a parent, a friend — instantly knows you're okay. Tapping it feels small on purpose: a little knock sound, a buzz, and the button flashes green for a second — that's the whole thing, no fireworks, no big screen, nothing else to do. A small, quiet tap is enough to tell the people who care about you that you're fine.

The "Knok"

Tap Knok whenever you want — once a day, a few times a day, however often suits you. Every tap instantly tells everyone watching over you that you're fine.

Watching over each other

You and someone you trust — a family member, a neighbour, a friend — both agree to watch each other. Nobody is added without approval on both sides.

When you add someone to watch, you choose how long you're comfortable going without hearing from them (a few hours, a day, longer — it's up to you). If they don't check in within that window, you're gently alerted so you know to reach out.

Adding people, the easy way

To watch someone, you add them as a contact — they get a request and approve it (and you approve theirs). Nothing is one-sided.

If they don't have knokkeroo yet, you can invite them straight from the app over WhatsApp. No separate sign-up link to find, no forms.

Know at a glance

Every contact you watch is shown with a simple colour: green means they checked in recently, amber means they're a little overdue, and red means they've missed their window. You don't have to dig for it — the colour tells you.

Quiet hours

Everyone sets their own "quiet hours" (like overnight) so a normal night's sleep is never mistaken for a missed check-in.

Your profile

Add a profile photo so the people watching over you recognise you at a glance. You can also choose to share your phone's battery level with them — handy context if your phone's about to die and you might miss a check-in. Both are entirely optional.

Your data, your control

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app. It's permanent and immediately removes your knoks, your contacts, and your profile.

Free or Unlimited — your choice

knokkeroo is free to use. Here's the difference between the two plans:

Free

Watch over up to 4 people, and be watched by as many as you like. No time limit, no trial — just free.

Unlimited — $4.99/month*

Watch over as many people as you want. Cancel any time from your phone's account settings.

*Price shown in USD; your store shows the exact price in your local currency.

One important note

knokkeroo is a simple way for people who care about each other to stay in touch. It is not a medical alert system or an emergency service, and it does not contact emergency services on your behalf.
