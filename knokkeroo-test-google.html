knokkeroo for Android

Live now, public

knokkeroo is publicly live on the Play Store — no tester group or sign-up needed anymore. Just search "knokkeroo" or use the button below.

As a tester, thank you for trying it early. If you're still on a testing-track install, you can keep using it as-is, or reinstall from the public listing below — either way you're on the same app and account.

Join the beta — one signup covers phone and watch

Open to anyone — no invitation needed

The beta on the phone is build 4.36.2. It adds the Home Screen widget, favourite contacts, notifications and the Wear OS watch app. Anyone can join — you do not need to be invited and you do not need to email anyone.

Android testers are the ones we need most. Every other platform has someone on it. This build has never been opened on a real Android phone by anyone, so anything you find — even something that looks too small to mention — is genuinely new information.

First, check which Google account you are using. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture (top right) and note the address shown. Both steps below must use that exact same account — this is the single most common reason the beta does not show up.

Step 1 · Join the tester group

Open groups.google.com/g/knokkeroo-testers and tap Ask to join (or accept, if you were already invited).

Step 2 · Become a tester on Google Play

Open play.google.com/apps/testing/com.goliontus.knokker and tap Become a tester.

Step 3 · Install on your phone

On that same page tap Download it on Google Play — or open the knokkeroo listing and tap Install (or Update, if you already have the public version). It is the same listing as the public app; once you are a tester it serves you the beta build instead, so there is no separate download to find.

If it says "not available" or "you're not a tester": check that Steps 1 and 2 used the same Google account, then wait — Google can take an hour or two to propagate access. Retry after that before reporting a problem.

Your Wear OS watch

Included in the same beta — nothing extra to join

The watch app works on Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch and other Wear OS watches. It is part of the same beta you joined above — there is no second signup, no second group and no separate tester list.

The watch app is an earlier build than the phone app. Google distributes it as its own package, and it has not been rebuilt alongside the newest phone releases. So expect the watch to be behind the phone — if something is on the phone and missing on the watch, that is known, and it is the phone build we most need tested.

Before you start: your watch must already be paired to your phone the normal way, through Galaxy Wearable (Samsung) or the Wear OS app. That is your watch's own setup and has nothing to do with knokkeroo. If your watch already shows your notifications, you are done with this part.

Step 1 · Install knokkeroo on your phone first

The watch app will not appear until the phone app is installed on the paired phone. Do the three steps above first.

Step 2 · Get it onto the watch

The watch app ships under the same Play listing as the phone app, so Google Play sends it to your watch for you. Give it a few minutes after installing on the phone.

If it has not arrived: on the watch, open the Play Store → Apps on your phone. knokkeroo will be in that list with an Install button. Tap it.

Step 3 · Pairing — there is none

There is no code to type and nothing to pair. The watch app has no login screen of its own. Your phone hands it your signed-in session automatically over the watch connection.

So all you do is: open knokkeroo on your phone once after installing, with the watch connected. Then open knokkeroo on the watch — you will already be signed in, and you will see your own status and a knok button.

If the watch says you are not signed in: open the app on your phone, confirm you are signed in there, then open the watch app again. The phone re-sends your session every time it starts and every time you sign in or out, so reopening the phone app is always the fix.

What this testing is for

The purpose of testing is to find errors. You are not here to confirm that knokkeroo works — you are here to find the places where it does not. A test that finds nothing tells us very little. A test that finds a problem is the most useful thing you can give us.

So please don't smooth anything over. If something looks wrong, reads oddly, arrives at a strange time, is hard to read, or simply confuses you, that counts — even if you're not sure it's a fault, and even if it feels too small to mention. Nothing is too small, and you can never report too much.

About the messages you receive

Every notification knokkeroo sends you during testing is part of the test. That includes reminders to knok, alerts that one of your contacts is overdue, invitations, and anything else that appears on your phone or watch.

They're sent so we can confirm they actually arrive, on time, on your device. So please don't switch them off or ignore them — and do judge them as if they were real. If a message arrives that you didn't expect, arrives at an odd hour, says something confusing, or doesn't arrive when you think it should, that is exactly what we need to hear about.

knokkeroo is not an emergency service, and nothing you receive while testing should be treated as an emergency alert.

What to test — on your phone

  1. Register / sign in. Enter your number, receive the verification code, sign in.
  2. knok. Tap the big button. Your own time should update immediately.
  3. Add a contact. Send a connection request; have them accept; check they appear.
  4. Accept a request. Approve an incoming request and check both sides now watch each other.
  5. Status colours. Check contacts show green (recent), amber (a little overdue), red (missed).
  6. Quiet hours. Set them, and check a normal night does not count as a missed check-in.
  7. Invite someone not on knokkeroo. Check they receive the WhatsApp invitation.
  8. Widget. Add the knokkeroo widget to your Home Screen; check it shows your status and updates after a knok.
  9. Account deletion. Me → Delete account. Check it signs you out and the account is gone.

What to test — on your watch

  1. It opens signed in. Open knokkeroo on the watch — no login screen, your own status is shown.
  2. knok from the watch. Tap knok. The watch should confirm it.
  3. The phone catches up on its own. Open the phone app — your new check-in should already be there, without you pulling to refresh.
  4. Out of range. Walk away from the phone (or turn Bluetooth off) and tap knok. It should tell you clearly rather than silently failing, and go through once reconnected.
  5. Signed out. Sign out on the phone, then open the watch app. It should say plainly that you are not signed in — it must not crash or show a blank screen.
  6. The watch face tile, if your watch shows tiles: check the knokkeroo tile appears and works.

How to report anything you find

Two ways, whichever suits you:

  • WhatsApp — +27 84 600 1188
  • Email — hello@knokkeroo.com

Both go straight to Charles, who builds knokkeroo — there's no support desk or queue in between, and he reads every one.

Please include, as far as you can:

  • What you did — the steps, in the order you did them
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened instead
  • When it happened — the date and roughly what time
  • Which device you were using
  • A screenshot, if there's anything to see

Don't worry about getting this perfect or writing it up neatly — a short message saying "this looked wrong at about 7 this morning" is far better than saying nothing. On WhatsApp a photo or a voice note is perfectly fine — you don't have to type it out.

If you're working from the downloadable test plan, put the test case ID (for example "CORE-3") in the subject line.
