Apple → iPhone

The main app. App Store id 6776208791, bundle id com.goliontus.knokker.

How to sign in — no SMS is sent, nothing can be charged.

  1. On the login screen open the country selector and choose United States (+1).
  2. Enter phone number 202 555 0100 and tap Send verification code.
  3. Enter code 148392 and tap Verify code.

A reserved demo account: the code is fixed, no message is ever sent, and it starts on the FREE plan so the paywall can be reviewed. Nine further demo accounts are listed in the reference section at the foot of this page.

What to test: sign in with the demo account above; tap Knok and watch your own time update; open the contacts list and check the status colours (green recently checked in, amber a little overdue, red missed); set quiet hours; then use Me → Delete account, which permanently removes the account.

What to review specifically — the paywall. The demo account starts FREE, so the first four contacts are visible and further contacts appear as anonymous locked tiles that open the paywall. Tapping Subscribe on a demo account is simulated: it unlocks everything with no transaction, no charge and no purchase sheet of any kind. Log out and back in to return to the free view and review it again. For real users, Subscribe opens Apple's own In-App Purchase sheet, which must be confirmed and can be cancelled. Nothing a reviewer taps can charge them.

Please note: knokkeroo is a personal communication tool, not a medical or emergency service, and never contacts emergency services. The in-app disclaimer and the Terms state this.

Apple → Apple Watch

The knokkeroo Apple Watch app is part of the same iPhone app (App Store id 6776208791) — there is no separate App Store listing or TestFlight build to install. It installs automatically alongside the phone app onto any paired Apple Watch. Note: the Watch companion ships from app version 4.x onwards. It is not present in the currently public App Store build (3.5.16); this section applies to whichever build carries it.

  1. Install and sign in to knokkeroo on the iPhone using the demo account in section 2 above.
  2. On the paired Apple Watch, open the Watch app (installed automatically — check the Watch's app list, or open the Watch app on iPhone and confirm knokkeroo shows as installed).
  3. Open knokkeroo on the Watch. It shows a single knok button.
  4. Tap knok on the Watch. Return to the iPhone app — the Me tile should update to reflect the new check-in.

Free feature: the Watch app itself is free on every plan. (Push notifications are a separate, paid/beta feature — not required to test the Watch app above.)

✓ End of the Apple Watch review section — you have everything you need to complete it. Nothing further on this page is required.

Apple → Apple TV ("knokkeroo TV")

knokkeroo TV is a separate app with its own App Store listing (App Store id 6795920763, bundle id com.goliontus.knokker.tv) and its own TestFlight build. It is a companion display only — it requires the iPhone app to be signed in first, then paired to it.

  1. Install knokkeroo on an iPhone or simulator. It is publicly available on the App Store (id 6776208791) — no TestFlight build is needed for this step. Sign in with the same demo account as section 2, repeated here so you need not scroll back: country United States (+1), phone 202 555 0100, code 148392. No SMS is sent.
  2. Install and open knokkeroo TV on the Apple TV / tvOS simulator. It displays a 6-digit pairing code.
  3. On the iPhone app, go to Me → Settings and choose Pair a TV, then enter the 6-digit code shown on the TV.
  4. The TV screen should then show the demo account's approved contacts and their check-in status tiles, matching what the phone app shows.

What to check specifically: the pairing flow above; that contact status tiles match the phone app; general Siri Remote navigation; and the app icon / Top Shelf image on the tvOS Home Screen.

Free feature: knokkeroo TV is free on every plan, same as the Watch app and widgets.

Google → Wear OS watch

The Wear OS app is the Android counterpart of the Apple Watch app above. It ships under the same package name as the phone app (com.goliontus.knokker) rather than as a separate listing, so Google Play delivers it to a paired watch alongside the phone app. Works on Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch and other Wear OS devices.

  1. Install knokkeroo on an Android phone and sign in with the demo account from section 2, repeated here: country United States (+1), phone 202 555 0100, code 148392. No SMS is sent.
  2. On a Wear OS watch already paired to that phone, open knokkeroo. If it has not installed itself, open the Play Store on the watch → Apps on your phone → install knokkeroo.
  3. There is no pairing step and no login screen on the watch. The phone pushes its signed-in session to the watch over the Wearable Data Layer automatically, so simply opening the phone app once is sufficient. This is the Android equivalent of the Apple Watch's app-group session sharing.
  4. The watch shows the demo account's current status and a knok button. Tapping it records a check-in, which appears in the phone app without a manual refresh.

What to check specifically: that the watch opens already signed in; that a watch-recorded knok reaches the phone; and that a signed-out state is shown clearly rather than crashing (sign out on the phone, then reopen the watch app).

Free feature: the Wear OS app is free on every plan, same as the Apple Watch app, the TV app and widgets. It never opens a purchase sheet.

You do not need anything below to complete a review; every device section above is self-contained. This is here only if you want more detail.

What the app does

knokkeroo is a simple daily check-in app. A user taps knok to let the people who care about them know they are okay. Contacts watch over each other through mutual, both-approved connections and are alerted if someone is overdue.

It is a personal communication tool — not a medical or emergency service. Please see the in-app disclaimer and our Terms for the full emergency disclaimer.

Suggested test steps (general)

  1. Contacts screen: after login you see a grid of tiles — green for recently checked in, amber for due, red for overdue, and some with a moon for quiet hours. Three tiles appear per row.
  2. knok: tap the knok tile. Your Me tile turns green and updates the time.
  3. Add a contact: tap Add Contact, then enter a name and a number. For demo accounts, no real message is sent.
  4. Respond to a request: tap a purple Approve tile to accept or decline a pending request.
  5. Timezones & quiet hours: the demo contacts are pinned to real timezones across the United States (Pacific, Mountain, Central, Eastern, Alaska) and Europe (London, Berlin, Athens). Each contact's time and quiet-hours moon reflect their own timezone, so they will not match your device's time. That is intentional, to show the feature. A normal, non-demo account instead follows the device's timezone.
  6. Settings: tap Me to change your name, knok interval, and quiet hours.
  7. Account deletion: open Me → Delete account. Demo accounts are protected and show a notice. Real accounts are permanently deleted. See our Account Deletion policy.

Notes for reviewers

  • No real SMS/WhatsApp is sent for demo accounts. Logging in and inviting demo contacts triggers no messages.
  • Demo data is reset by us between reviews, so the demo accounts always start in a known state.
  • Demo contacts keep fixed US/European timezones. We never overwrite them. Their displayed times differ from yours on purpose so that timezone and quiet-hours behaviour can be reviewed.
  • The app targets adults and is not directed at children.
  • Subscriptions & billing: the free tier fully shows a user's first four contacts; a paid subscription shows all of them. The subscription is sold and billed entirely by the platform's own store — Apple In-App Purchase on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android — and we never see or store card details. On the Huawei AppGallery build there is no billing at all: every contact is visible, free. All demo accounts start on the FREE plan. Tapping Subscribe on a demo account is simulated — it unlocks everything with no charge and no store purchase sheet at all; log out and back in to reset to the free view. For real (non-demo) users, Subscribe opens that platform's own cancellable purchase sheet. No purchase is required to review the app, and nothing a reviewer taps can charge them.
