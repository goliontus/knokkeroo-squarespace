Apple Watch

Beta — rides along inside the iPhone TestFlight build

Requires the iPhone TestFlight beta first (see the iPhone page, Option B) and an Apple Watch paired to that iPhone, watchOS 9+. Watch is a paid/beta-plan feature.

Register

  1. Join the iPhone beta first: testflight.apple.com/join/XaabanK9.

Install

  1. On the iPhone's Watch app (My Watch tab), find knokkeroo under Available Apps.
  2. Install it on the Watch if it hasn't installed automatically.

What to test

  • Watch shows your current status on open
  • Tapping Knok on the Watch records a check-in
  • The iPhone app reflects a Watch-recorded Knok without needing a manual refresh
  • Behaviour when signed out (should show a clear "not signed in" state, not crash)

Download full test plan (.docx) — Section 3 covers Apple Watch in ISO test-case format.

Apple TV

Beta — separate TestFlight app, build 4.21.0 (29)

Apple TV is a separate app in App Store Connect (its own TestFlight beta, not bundled with the iPhone app). Requires an Apple TV on tvOS 16+, and a phone already signed into knokkeroo to complete pairing.

Register

  1. On the Apple TV (or a device signed into the same Apple ID), open testflight.apple.com/join/72H1DdnT.
  2. Accept the invite.

Install

  1. Install knokkeroo TV via TestFlight on the Apple TV.
  2. Open it — it will show a 6-digit pairing code.

What to test

  • TV shows a 6-digit pairing code on first open
  • On the phone app (already signed in): Me → Pair a TV → enter the code
  • TV screen advances to the main status screen once paired
  • The big "I'M OKAY" button on the TV records a Knok (free — not gated behind a paid plan)
  • Shared-screen awareness: anyone who can see the TV can see your contacts' check-in status — read the Terms' Connected Devices clause below before pairing a shared/communal TV

Download full test plan (.docx) — Section 4 covers Apple TV in ISO test-case format.
