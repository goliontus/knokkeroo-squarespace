What this testing is for

The purpose of testing is to find errors. You are not here to confirm that knokkeroo works — you are here to find the places where it does not. A test that finds nothing tells us very little. A test that finds a problem is the most useful thing you can give us.

So please don't smooth anything over. If something looks wrong, reads oddly, arrives at a strange time, is hard to read, or simply confuses you, that counts — even if you're not sure it's a fault, and even if it feels too small to mention. Nothing is too small, and you can never report too much.

About the messages you receive

Every notification knokkeroo sends you during testing is part of the test. That includes reminders to knok, alerts that one of your contacts is overdue, invitations, and anything else that appears on your phone, watch or TV.

They're sent so we can confirm they actually arrive, on time, on your device. So please don't switch them off or ignore them — and do judge them as if they were real. If a message arrives that you didn't expect, arrives at an odd hour, says something confusing, or doesn't arrive when you think it should, that is exactly what we need to hear about.

knokkeroo is not an emergency service, and nothing you receive while testing should be treated as an emergency alert.

How to report anything you find

Email hello@knokkeroo.com. It goes straight to Charles, who builds knokkeroo — there's no support desk or queue in between, and he reads every one.

Please include, as far as you can:

  • What you did — the steps, in the order you did them
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened instead
  • When it happened — the date and roughly what time
  • Which device you were using
  • A screenshot or photo, if there's anything to see

Don't worry about getting this perfect or writing it up neatly — a short message saying "this looked wrong at about 7 this morning" is far better than saying nothing. If you'd rather report it some other way than email, say so in a short email and Charles will set that up with you.
