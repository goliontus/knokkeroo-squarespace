knokkeroo for Garmin

On the Connect IQ store — beta

knokkeroo is now on the Garmin Connect IQ store as a beta app. It works on 135 Garmin watch models, including the fēnix, Forerunner, Venu, Instinct, epix, vívoactive, MARQ, Descent, Approach and tactix families.

Right now, only the owner's Garmin account can install it

That is a Garmin rule, not a choice: a Connect IQ beta app can only be downloaded by the developer account that uploaded it. It cannot be shared with other testers yet.

Once it has been checked on a real watch it will be released publicly, and then anyone with a Garmin watch can install it from the Connect IQ store in the normal way. The steps below will not change — only who is allowed to do step 2.

Install it on your watch

  1. Make sure your watch is paired to your phone in the Garmin Connect app and is showing as connected.
  2. Open the app's own page in the Connect IQ store: apps.garmin.com/apps/a251aae1-5b9b-49f9-82d3-72691bfedafc, signed in with the Garmin account that owns the app. Open that link on your phone and it hands over to the Connect IQ app. There is no "Beta Apps" list to browse — a beta app is reached by its own link.
  3. Tap Install, and pick your watch. The watch must be nearby and connected. Installing takes a minute or two and the watch may restart the app list afterwards.
  4. On the watch, hold the middle-left button to open the app list (or press START and scroll), and open knokkeroo.

Your phone needs the v4 test version — the store version cannot pair yet

Pair a Garmin does not exist in the knokkeroo you get from the App Store, Google Play or the AppGallery today. It is part of v4, which is still in testing. If you install the normal store version and go looking for it under Profile, you will not find it, and nothing is wrong with your watch.

Get the v4 test version of the phone app first, from the testing pages — pick your phone there (iPhone, Android or Huawei) and follow that page. Then come back here and carry on below.

Connect it to your knokkeroo account

  1. On your phone, make sure you are running the v4 test version of knokkeroo, not the one from the store — see the note just above.
  2. The first time you open it, the watch shows a 6-digit code and the words “Enter this in the app: Profile → Pair a Garmin”. If it says it could not get a code, press the button to try again — the watch needs your phone's connection to reach the internet.
  3. On your phone, open the knokkeroo app, go to Profile, and find Pair a Garmin.
  4. Type the 6-digit code from the watch and confirm. The watch checks every few seconds and switches itself to the knok screen once it is accepted. You only do this once per watch.

What to test

  1. It opens. The screen shows the kangaroo with the time of your last check-in underneath. There is no wordmark — the kangaroo is the button.
  2. Press the button to knok. Confirm the time underneath updates, and that the same check-in appears on your phone within a few seconds.
  3. Press it twice. That should record two check-ins — pressing twice means you checked in twice, and the app is right to record both. What must never happen is one press turning into two because a send was retried. The watch only reuses a send while it is still waiting for an answer.
  4. Try it with your phone out of range (leave the phone in another room). It should tell you it could not send, rather than pretending it worked.
  5. Reopen the app later. It should still be paired and go straight to the kangaroo screen, not ask for a code again.

What this testing is for

The purpose of testing is to find errors. You are not here to confirm that knokkeroo works — you are here to find the places where it does not. A test that finds nothing tells us very little. A test that finds a problem is the most useful thing you can give us.

So please don't smooth anything over. If something looks wrong, reads oddly, arrives at a strange time, is hard to read, or simply confuses you, that counts — even if you're not sure it's a fault, and even if it feels too small to mention. Nothing is too small, and you can never report too much.

About the messages you receive

Every notification knokkeroo sends you during testing is part of the test. That includes reminders to knok, alerts that one of your contacts is overdue, invitations, and anything else that appears on your phone or watch.

They're sent so we can confirm they actually arrive, on time, on your device. So please don't switch them off or ignore them — and do judge them as if they were real. If a message arrives that you didn't expect, arrives at an odd hour, says something confusing, or doesn't arrive when you think it should, that is exactly what we need to hear about.

knokkeroo is not an emergency service, and nothing you receive while testing should be treated as an emergency alert.

How to report anything you find

Two ways, whichever suits you:

  • WhatsApp — +27 84 600 1188
  • Email — hello@knokkeroo.com

Both go straight to Charles, who builds knokkeroo — there's no support desk or queue in between, and he reads every one.

Please include, as far as you can:

  • What you did — the steps, in the order you did them
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened instead
  • When it happened — the date and roughly what time
  • Your watch model, and the phone you had it paired with
  • A photo of the watch screen — worth far more than a description

Don't worry about getting this perfect or writing it up neatly — a short message saying "this looked wrong at about 7 this morning" is far better than saying nothing. On WhatsApp a photo or a voice note is perfectly fine — you don't have to type it out.
